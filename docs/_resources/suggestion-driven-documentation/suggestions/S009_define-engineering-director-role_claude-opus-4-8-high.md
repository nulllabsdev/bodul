## Issue

Line 125 (Applying suggestions, step 4) says "If there are any questions for the
engineering director, ask them." This is the only occurrence of "engineering
director" in the document; every other role reference uses "reviewer." The term
is never defined, so a reader cannot tell whether the engineering director is
the same person as the reviewer, a separate approver, or an arbitrary
stakeholder.

## Suggestion

Make the role vocabulary consistent. Either define "engineering director" once
(stating how the role relates to the reviewer), or replace it with the
already-established "reviewer" term. Replacing it is the simpler fix unless the
workflow genuinely needs a second, higher-authority role at the apply step.
